# General Machines

**General Machines is an AI research lab studying how AI agents buy.**

AI agents are starting to shop for people, but online stores were built for customers who can see. We record what really happens when agents shop, and turn it into data, benchmarks and small models for the agentic economy.

## [Machine Commerce](https://machinecommerce.co/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org_readme)

**Machine Commerce is our first research arm.** We run shopping agents in real stores, judge every journey, and find which store details decide whether an agent can buy, and whether it chooses you.

- **For AI labs and agent builders:** real commerce traces and evaluations to train and test shopping agents.
- **For merchants:** what to change so AI agents can find, understand and buy from your store.
- **For retailers:** applied AI for shelves, catalogs, ads and storefronts, in production with our retail customers.

## What we're releasing

| | Release | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **CartTrace** | Real shopping journeys by AI agents, recorded end to end: search, compare, cart, checkout. The first release, CartTrace-100k, has 100,000 traces. |
| **2** | **CartBench** | A benchmark for commerce agents, built from CartTrace. |
| **3** | **Commerce&#8209;1** | A small model, trained on CartTrace, that spots when a shopping agent is about to fail. |

Releases will appear here as they ship. Until then, most of our work is private.

## Beyond commerce

Commerce is where we start. General Machines builds datasets for agents that act, on the web and in the physical world, and small models that run close to the data, with no cloud dependency.
